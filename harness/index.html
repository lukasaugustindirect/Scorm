    SCORM 1.2

    • LMSInitialize once on open, LMSFinish once on close.
    • cmi.core.lesson_status should leave not attempted immediately, then reach completed (or passed with a mastery score).
    • cmi.core.session_time must look like 00:01:23, not a number of seconds.
    • cmi.suspend_data must stay under 4096 characters.
    • cmi.core.exit should be suspend if you close the course part-way, and empty once it is complete.

    SCORM 2004

    • Calls lose the LMS prefix: Initialize, Terminate.
    • cmi.completion_status and cmi.success_status are separate.
    • cmi.score.scaled runs 0–1, not 0–100.
    • cmi.session_time must be an ISO 8601 duration such as PT1M23S.
    • Set launch mode to Browse or Review: nothing should be recorded beyond the bare session.

    xAPI and cmi5

    • cmi5 requires initialized first and terminated last.
    • completed, passed and failed must carry a duration.
    • Every cmi5 statement must carry the .../context/categories/cmi5 category, and any statement with completion or success must also carry .../moveon.
    • The session id from LMS.LaunchData's contextTemplate must appear on every statement.
    • Statement ids must be UUIDs.
    • In Browse or Review mode only initialized and terminated are allowed.
